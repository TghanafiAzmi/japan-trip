GALA Yuzawa Snow Resort (Gondola, Sledding & Play)

The train platform exits directly inside the GALA Yuzawa Ski Center (Cowabunga Station)! No bus or walking required. Rent snow boots, gloves, and sleds on 2F. Board the 8-person Diligence Gondola right inside the terminal up to the mountain alpine area (Cheers, 800m elevation). Sled down snow slopes, take photos against snow peaks, and enjoy hot cocoa.
